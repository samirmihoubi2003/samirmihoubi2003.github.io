Customer Retention Analytics

From transaction records to a clearer picture of customer value.

The data problem

Online Retail II contains line-item transactions, cancellations, missing customer identifiers and duplicate records. Raw sales are not yet decision-ready customer analytics.

My approach

Designed a pandas cleaning workflow, produced orders and customer-level data, developed RFM segmentation and monthly cohort retention, and modeled outputs in PostgreSQL for BI exploration.

Results and interpretation

The Champions segment contains 1,473 customers and accounts for approximately 69% of analyzed sales. This is a result from a historical public dataset, not revenue generated for an employer.

Methodological note

Cleaning rules exclude cancellations and anonymous transactions from the customer-level positive-sales analysis. The sales total of approximately £17.37M applies to that defined analytical scope.
